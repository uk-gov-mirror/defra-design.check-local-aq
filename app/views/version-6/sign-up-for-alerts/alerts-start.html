Set up air pollution alerts

Get a free alert by text message or email when air pollution is forecast to be raised in a place you choose.

You can choose to get alerts about:

  • nitrogen dioxide
  • PM10
  • PM2.5
  • ozone
  • sulphur dioxide

You can also choose the pollution levels you want to be told about – moderate, high or very high.

Alerts include health advice and what you can do to reduce your exposure. You can change or stop your alerts at any time.
